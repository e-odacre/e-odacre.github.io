Church Websites

Lightweight church websites produced through the Agency workflow, with hands-on review and refinement.

Research, AI-assisted production, and manual QA — finished website work, rather than complex web applications.

Problem

Church websites need to present useful information clearly and look professional without unnecessary complexity.

Approach

I used the Agency application to research and produce the sites, then personally reviewed the generated output and made the changes needed for a finished website.

Workflow

Research → Generate → Review → Refine → Finished website

What I built

  • Researched the churches and the information their websites needed to communicate.
  • Produced lightweight HTML/CSS sites through an AI-assisted website workflow.
  • Manually reviewed and refined design, content presentation, usability, and web best practices.
  • Reviewed performance considerations and kept the implementation lightweight.

Why it matters

The value is in turning generated output into a usable finished site. Human review connects the speed of assisted production with the judgment needed for clear, appropriate presentation.

Technology

HTML, CSS, and the Agency production workflow.

What was produced

Produced and reviewed lightweight church websites. This work demonstrates practical production and QA; it does not claim complex application architecture or measured performance gains.
