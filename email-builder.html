    A small window into the full system.

    This demo uses browser-side HTML templates inspired by the library's welcome and product-launch recipes. The full workspace uses Laravel, Nunjucks and MJML, with schema checks, strict compilation and Klaviyo template-tag preservation. The checks here cover copy, links, color contrast and output size; they do not run that engine or verify inbox compatibility. Sample brand and copy are fictional.
