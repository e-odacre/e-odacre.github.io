About me

A developer's eye.
A marketer's curiosity.

I started in web development and moved into email marketing because I like how it brings design, copy, and technology together. My development background lets me build the tools around a campaign as well as the email itself.

I've also worked as a chef, tutor, and in construction. Those roles taught me to stay organized, explain things clearly, and solve practical problems with other people.

How I work

Understand. Build. Review.

  1. Understand & research

    Start with the audience, marketing problem, and existing workflow before choosing a tool.

  2. Design & build

    Turn the brief into a campaign or system, automating repetitive work where it helps.

  3. Test & improve

    Check the details and refine the output. AI-assisted work gets the same hands-on review as anything else I build.
