About me

I started in web development and moved into email marketing because I liked how it brings design, copy, and technology together. I enjoy building things, but I also care about what they communicate and who they're for. My development background lets me go beyond the email platform: I can build the tools and workflows around a campaign, as well as the email itself.

What I do

From the message in the inbox to the workflow behind it, these are connected parts of my work.

Email marketing

Campaigns, lifecycle flows, copy, design, and responsive email development. I work with Klaviyo and Mailchimp, translating ideas into branded emails with Figma, HTML/CSS, and MJML, then reviewing the output before handoff.

Marketing automation

Lead research, enrichment, qualification, and repeatable marketing workflows. I connect data sources and APIs, use AI-assisted research where it helps, and automate repetitive steps.

Development

Internal tools and marketing systems built with Laravel, PHP, JavaScript, and databases. This is how I turn a useful workflow into something that can be run, reviewed, and improved.

Experience

Beyond marketing
